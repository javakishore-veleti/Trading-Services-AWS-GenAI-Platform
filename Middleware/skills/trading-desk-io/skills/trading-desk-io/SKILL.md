# FOREX trading desk international operations

This skill is the international book of the FOREX trading desk. Catalog, offer, and order are one business here.

Use it only for these countries:

- South Korea
- Japan
- China
- Australia
- New Zealand
- Brazil
- Belgium
- France

For a customer in one of these countries, a request can select the product, price the offer, and place, cancel, upgrade, or downgrade the order in the same flow. Before the price, take a new mid when a data release is scheduled for that country or for the other currency in the pair. A pair that is not in the catalog is not a price. Upgrade and downgrade keep the order timings: immediate cancel of the existing order, or bill-through until a date.

Outside these countries, do not use this skill. A view of the pair's chart does not replace the rating, the spread, or the all-in.
