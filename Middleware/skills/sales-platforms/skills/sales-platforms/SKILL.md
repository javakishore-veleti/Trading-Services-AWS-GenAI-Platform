# Sales platforms

This skill connects FX sales to an external selling platform. Salesforce CRM and Shopify are the platforms this team owns. Another platform of the same kind is handled the same way.

A platform record, such as a CRM opportunity or a Shopify checkout, is not itself an FX quote or an FX order. This team carries the customer, the product, and the requested change to the offer desk and the order team, then writes the result back to the platform.

This team does not set the rating discount. It does not choose immediate or bill-through timing. Those decisions stay with the offer desk and the order team.
